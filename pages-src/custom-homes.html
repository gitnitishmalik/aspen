Custom homes

Planned for the land, built for the long run.

Before we build, we study the site: access, drainage, foundation needs, views, sun and where the utilities will run. Then we plan how you'll use the home, from outdoor rooms and garage placement to guest space, storage and upkeep.

  • Scope and finish level defined before we estimate
  • Site and foundation work coordinated with the build
  • Proportion, storage, natural light and flow designed in
  • Steady communication from the first sketch to the keys

Luxury farmhouses

Farmhouse character, modern comfort.

A great farmhouse balances open gathering spaces with private rooms, natural light, storage and outdoor living. We blend wood, stone, metal roofing and statement lighting with the insulation, drainage and structure that keep it performing for decades.

  • Porches, entries, kitchens and storage planned around your routines
  • Finishes chosen for both style and durability
  • Trim, cabinetry and fixtures coordinated so it feels whole

Barndominiums

Strength of a shell, finish of a custom home.

The best barndominiums are planned before the shell goes up: insulation, rooflines, the slab, interior walls and mechanical systems. We lay out ceiling heights, bedroom privacy, the kitchen, shop access and light together, then finish the inside with the care of a custom home.

  • Slab, shell, insulation and utilities reviewed before construction
  • Open living, private rooms, storage and shop access planned as one
  • Guest suites, offices, hobby rooms and covered outdoor living
